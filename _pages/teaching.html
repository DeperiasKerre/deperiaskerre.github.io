--- layout: archive title: "Teaching" permalink: /teaching/ author_profile: true --- # Teaching I am a Lecturer at the School of Computing and Engineering Sciences, Strathmore University, Kenya, where I teach undergraduate and graduate courses in computer science, information technology, artificial intelligence, and data management. I am also actively involved in student mentorship, project supervision, and postgraduate research guidance. ## Courses ### Undergraduate Courses * Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning * Advanced Database Systems * Database Systems * Web Application Development * Introduction to Computer Programming (C++) * Fundamentals of Computers * IT Fundamentals * Information Systems in Financial Services * Project Supervision ### Graduate Courses * Advanced Database Systems * Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning * Research Project Supervision * Graduate Research Methods and Mentorship ## Supervision and Guidance ### MSc Thesis Supervision I supervise Master's research projects in areas including: * Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning * Information Retrieval * Knowledge Representation * Information Extraction * Data Integration * Data Analytics * Semantic Technologies ### PhD Thesis Co-supervision I co-supervise doctoral research in topics related to: * Artificial Intelligence * Knowledge Graphs * Semantic Data Integration * Information Extraction * Metadata Quality * Information Retrieval ### PhD Thesis Supervision I am interested in supervising doctoral research in the following areas: * Knowledge Representation and Reasoning * Machine Learning for Knowledge Representation * Information Retrieval and Dataset Discovery * Metadata Quality Assessment * Semantic Keyword Quality * Knowledge Graph Engineering * Context-Aware Data Integration * Machine Learning for Structured and Unstructured Data * FAIR Data and Data Interoperability ## Student Mentorship In addition to formal supervision, I provide academic and career mentorship to undergraduate and postgraduate students pursuing research and professional development in computing and data science.
